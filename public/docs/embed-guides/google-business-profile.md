# Adding Book-IT to your Google Business Profile

Google Business Profile (the panel that appears in Google Search and Maps) does
**not** support embedded HTML. Instead you add your booking URL as a link, so
the **Book / Appointments** button points to Book-IT.

## Add a booking / appointment link

1. Search your business name on Google while signed in to the Google account
   that manages the profile (or open the Google Business Profile manager).
2. In the profile editing tools, find **Edit profile → Booking** (sometimes shown
   as **Appointment links** or **Bookings**).
3. Add the URL:

   ```
   https://app.bookit.monoliet.cloud/book/YOUR-SLUG
   ```

4. Save. Google may take some time to review and show the button.

## Add it as the website or a post link (fallback)

If the Booking field isn't available for your business category:

- Add the booking URL as a **post** ("Book now" call-to-action), or
- Use it as a secondary action link.

## Notes

- Replace `YOUR-SLUG` with your real slug (Book-IT dashboard → Widget tab).
- For Dutch customers, you can use `…/book/YOUR-SLUG?lang=nl`.
- The Appointment/Booking link option depends on your business category and
  region; if it's missing, the website/post link is the reliable fallback.
