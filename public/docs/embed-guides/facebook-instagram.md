# Adding Book-IT to Facebook & Instagram

Facebook and Instagram don't allow embedded HTML on your profile, but both let
you add a **booking link / action button** that opens your Book-IT widget.

Your booking URL:

```
https://app.bookit.monoliet.cloud/book/YOUR-SLUG
```

## Facebook Page — "Book Now" button

1. Go to your Facebook **Page**.
2. Under the cover photo, click the action button (or **+ Add a button**).
3. Choose **Book now / Book with you** → **Link to website**.
4. Paste your booking URL and save.

## Instagram — Action button or link in bio

**Action button (business/creator accounts):**

1. Open your Instagram profile → **Edit profile**.
2. Tap **Action buttons** (or *Contact options → Action buttons*).
3. Choose a booking partner if listed, otherwise use the link methods below.

**Link in bio:**

1. **Edit profile → Website** (or **Links**), and paste your booking URL.
2. Or add it to a link-in-bio page (Linktree, etc.) as "Book an appointment".

**Stories & posts:** add a **Link sticker** in a Story, or put the URL in posts'
captions, pointing to your booking URL.

## Notes

- Replace `YOUR-SLUG` with your real slug (Book-IT dashboard → Widget tab).
- For Dutch customers: `…/book/YOUR-SLUG?lang=nl`.
- Available buttons depend on your account type (business/creator) and region.
