# Book-IT — Legal & GDPR Templates

Starter legal documents for Book-IT and its business customers ("tenants").
These are **templates**, not legal advice — review with qualified counsel
before relying on them. Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]` with your details.

| File | Purpose | Parties |
|---|---|---|
| [dpa-en.md](./dpa-en.md) / [dpa-nl.md](./dpa-nl.md) | Data Processing Agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) | Book-IT (processor) ↔ tenant (controller) |
| [privacy-policy-nl.md](./privacy-policy-nl.md) | Privacy statement a tenant publishes to its customers | Tenant ↔ its clients |
| [terms-of-service-en.md](./terms-of-service-en.md) / [terms-of-service-nl.md](./terms-of-service-nl.md) | Terms of Service for using Book-IT | Book-IT ↔ tenant |
| [sub-processor-register.md](./sub-processor-register.md) | List of sub-processors Book-IT uses | Reference |

## Common placeholders

- `[BUSINESS_NAME]`, `[BUSINESS_LEGAL_NAME]` — the tenant's business name
- `[KVK_NUMBER]` — Dutch Chamber of Commerce number
- `[BUSINESS_ADDRESS]`, `[BUSINESS_EMAIL]`, `[BUSINESS_WEBSITE]`
- `[CONTACT_EMAIL]` — privacy/DPO contact
- `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`
- `[PROCESSOR_LEGAL_NAME]` — the Book-IT operating entity (e.g. Monoliet)
- `[PROCESSOR_KVK]`, `[PROCESSOR_ADDRESS]`, `[PROCESSOR_EMAIL]`
