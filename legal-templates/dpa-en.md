# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Pursuant to Article 28 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / EU 2016/679)**

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between:

- **Controller:** [BUSINESS_LEGAL_NAME], KVK [KVK_NUMBER], [BUSINESS_ADDRESS] ("Controller"); and
- **Processor:** [PROCESSOR_LEGAL_NAME], KVK [PROCESSOR_KVK], [PROCESSOR_ADDRESS], operator of the Book-IT platform ("Processor").

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE_DATE].

## 1. Subject matter and roles

The Processor provides the Book-IT appointment-booking platform. In doing so it
processes personal data on behalf of the Controller. The Controller determines
the purposes and means of processing; the Processor acts only on the
Controller's documented instructions, including this DPA and its use of the
platform's settings.

## 2. Duration

This DPA applies for as long as the Processor processes personal data on behalf
of the Controller under the main service agreement.

## 3. Nature and purpose of processing

Hosting and operating an online booking system: capturing appointment requests,
managing bookings and customer records, sending booking-related notifications
(email/WhatsApp), and processing payments via the configured payment provider.

## 4. Categories of data subjects

- The Controller's customers / clients (and guest bookers)
- The Controller's staff members
- The Controller's account administrators (agents)

## 5. Categories of personal data

- Identification & contact data: name, email, phone number
- Booking data: appointment date/time, selected services, notes
- Optional data the Controller chooses to collect (e.g. reference images,
  service address, age confirmation)
- Payment metadata (status, method, amounts) — card data is handled by the
  payment provider, not stored by the Processor
- Account/authentication data for staff and agents

No special-category data is required by the platform. If the Controller chooses
to enter such data (e.g. health-related notes), it does so as controller and at
its own responsibility.

## 6. Processor obligations

The Processor shall:

1. Process personal data only on the Controller's documented instructions.
2. Ensure persons authorised to process the data are bound by confidentiality.
3. Implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (Clause 9).
4. Respect the conditions for engaging sub-processors (Clause 7).
5. Assist the Controller, taking into account the nature of processing, in
   responding to data-subject requests (Chapter III GDPR).
6. Assist the Controller with Articles 32–36 GDPR (security, breach
   notification, DPIAs), taking into account the information available.
7. At the Controller's choice, delete or return all personal data after the end
   of services, and delete existing copies unless retention is legally required.
8. Make available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with
   Article 28 and allow for and contribute to audits (Clause 10).

The Processor shall inform the Controller if, in its opinion, an instruction
infringes the GDPR or other data-protection law.

## 7. Sub-processors

The Controller grants general authorisation for the Processor to engage
sub-processors listed in the **Sub-processor Register**
(sub-processor-register.md). The Processor shall:

- impose data-protection obligations on each sub-processor no less protective
  than this DPA; and
- inform the Controller of intended changes (additions/replacements), giving the
  Controller the opportunity to object within 30 days. If the Controller
  reasonably objects, the parties will discuss in good faith; the Controller may
  terminate the affected service if no resolution is reached.

## 8. International transfers

Personal data is hosted in the EU (see the Sub-processor Register). Where any
transfer outside the EEA occurs, it shall be covered by an adequacy decision,
EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or another valid Chapter V transfer mechanism.

## 9. Security measures

Taking into account the state of the art and the risks, the Processor maintains
measures including:

- Encryption in transit (TLS) and encryption at rest of the hosted database
- Role-based access control and tenant data isolation (row-level security)
- Secrets/credentials stored outside source code, in environment configuration
- Access restricted to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis
- Regular backups of the production database
- Logging of relevant access and administrative actions

## 10. Audits

The Processor shall make available information needed to demonstrate compliance
and allow audits by the Controller or an appointed auditor, no more than once a
year (or after a security incident), on reasonable notice, during business
hours, and subject to confidentiality. The Processor may satisfy audit requests
by providing relevant third-party certifications or reports where available.

## 11. Personal data breaches

The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event
within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the
Controller's data, providing the information the Controller needs to meet its
Article 33/34 obligations.

## 12. Liability and miscellaneous

Liability is governed by the main service agreement. This DPA is governed by the
laws of the Netherlands; disputes are subject to the competent court in the
Processor's district, unless mandatory law provides otherwise. If any provision
conflicts with the main agreement, this DPA prevails on data-protection matters.
